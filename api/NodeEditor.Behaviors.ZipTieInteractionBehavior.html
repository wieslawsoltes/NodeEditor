Remarks

Attach before drawing selection behaviors. Drag a collar to move one anchor; Shift-drag a collar or the bundled span to translate the entire bundle. Double-click the span to insert a collar and an intermediate collar to remove it. Ctrl/Cmd+J creates a bundle; Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+J dissolves the selected bundle. Escape rolls back an active drag. A completed drag is one undo operation.
